The 8-Track Immigrant Life Architecture

4 live production engines active in today's demo, backed by complete blueprints across all 8 APTA problem areas.

1. School Enrollment & Schedules Live + Blueprint
  • 1A: Enrollment Document Wizard: Child age/district intake $\rightarrow$ state immunization gap analysis & lease proof checklist.
  • 1B: School Calendar Extractor (EduBridge): [LIVE] Flyer to Telugu schedule + 1-click .ICS export.
2. Forms & Official Documents Live + Blueprint
  • 2A: AI Government Form Explainer: Box-by-box guide explaining W-4s, lunch waivers & county exemptions in plain Telugu.
  • 2B: "What Does This Letter Mean?" (Decodr): [LIVE] 3-card decision matrix + penalty warnings + counter-email.
3. Language Barriers at Doctor Visits Live + Blueprint
  • 3A: Pre-Visit Phrasebook & Q&A Checklist: Side-by-side English/Telugu questions for patients to ask and anticipate at checkup.
  • 3B: Doctor Visit Triage Card (ArogyaCard): [LIVE] 3-tap symptoms $\rightarrow$ US nurse triage sheet with Fullscreen mode.
4. DMV & Transportation Steps Live + Blueprint
  • 4A: License Document Auditor (DMVReady): [LIVE] Visa rules engine flagging mandatory SSA-L676 letter.
  • 4B: Immigrant Public Transit Explainer: Step-by-step local bus/train directions, Telugu fare guide & stop etiquette.
5. Community Resources Architecture Ready
  • 5A: Safe-Aid Chatbot: Free clinics, food pantries, and ESL classes filtered by "No SSN Required" and "Public Charge Safe".
  • 5B: Zip Code Starter Kit Generator: 1-page printable immigrant welcome kit indexing nearby libraries, pantries, and temples.
6. Immigrant-Owned Businesses Architecture Ready
  • 6A: Bilingual Business Directory: Hyper-local search for regional home services, tailors, and caterers by language spoken.
  • 6B: Small Business Registration Guide: Step-by-step LLC filing, free IRS EIN registration, sales tax permits, and EAD compliance.
7. Family Communication Architecture Ready
  • 7A: Generational Bridge Messenger: Slang and idiom translation that preserves emotional warmth (*Vatsalyam*) between elders and teens.
  • 7B: Shared Bilingual Family Board: High-contrast, large-font calendar and chore coordinator displaying English and Telugu side-by-side.
8. Stories & Traditions Architecture Ready
  • 8A: Katha Oral Folklore Keeper: 1-tap voice recording transformed into illustrated English/Telugu digital storybook cards.
  • 8B: Spoken Recipe & Tradition Archiver: Conversational cooking instructions converted into standardized illustrated recipe cards with US grocery swaps.
